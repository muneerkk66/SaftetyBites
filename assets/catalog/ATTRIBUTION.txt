Source database: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/data
Open Food Facts contributors: https://world.openfoodfacts.org

SafeBiteAI filters the Open Food Facts export to UK products with usable
ingredient or allergen evidence, selects a limited set of fields, maps allergen
tags to SafeBiteAI categories, and serialises the result as compressed JSONL.
The resulting adapted database is offered under ODbL 1.0. The transformation
method is published at:
https://github.com/muneerkk66/SaftetyBites/blob/main/scripts/build_offline_catalog.py

Open Food Facts does not endorse SafeBiteAI. Community data may be incomplete
or outdated. Always verify the current product label.
