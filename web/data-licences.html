Open Food Facts

Contains information from Open Food Facts, which is made available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0. Individual database contents are available under the Database Contents Licence (DbCL) 1.0. Product images are provided by Open Food Facts contributors under CC BY-SA 3.0; packaging may contain additional third-party rights.

SafeBiteAI filters the Open Food Facts export to UK products with ingredient or allergen evidence, selects a limited set of fields, maps allergen tags and serialises the result as compressed JSONL. This adapted database is offered under ODbL 1.0.

Food Standards Agency

Food hygiene ratings and food alerts contain Food Standards Agency Crown copyright information used under the Open Government Licence v3.0. The sources are the Food Hygiene Rating Scheme API and the Food Alerts API.

Ratings can change. SafeBiteAI shows the inspection date supplied by the API and retrieves live results when the user searches. Always follow the linked official recall notice and verify affected product, batch and date information.

Postcodes.io

Postcode coordinates are supplied by Postcodes.io from the open datasets identified in its licence documentation.
